    * It ends at a fold (saddle-node of limit cycles) at g₂*/2π = 97.2 MHz. The slow pair meets its own conjugate on the real axis at radius 0.972, and one multiplier then crosses +1, within Δε_p ~ 10⁻⁶ of the fold. This is a Hamiltonian saddle-centre unfolded by the loss, not a Hamiltonian–Hopf collision: the fast (buffer-like) pair never meets the slow pair. The Floquet sweep could not go past ε_p ≈ 0.786 because no cat solution exists beyond it. Pseudo-arclength continuation follows the branch through the fold.
    * Fold line: g₂*|α|/2π ≈ 219 MHz, i.e. g₂* ∝ 1/|α|, constant to 1% for ε_d/g₂ from 5.25 to 13.5. There is no fold below g₂ max for ε_d/2g₂ ≤ 2.5.
